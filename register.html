Who our support is for

You're welcome to apply if your school:

  • has limited access to quality contemporary instruments
  • wants to grow or start a rock or contemporary music program
  • can provide a safe, supervised space for instruments to be used
  • is committed to giving as many students as possible access to music

We prioritise schools where instruments will make the biggest difference, often those with an ICSEA around or below the national average (1000). But every school will hear back from us with a clear outcome and explanation.
