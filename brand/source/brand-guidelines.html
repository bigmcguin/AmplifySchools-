The short version

If you only read one page, read this one. These six rules cover most of what you'll make for Amplify Schools.

1

Say our name in full

We're Amplify Schools. Two words, both with capitals. Not "Amplify" and not "AS".

2

Use the logo files as they are

Pick the colour, white or navy version from the logo folder. Never redraw, recolour, stretch or crop it.

3

Navy, teal and plenty of white

Navy does the heavy lifting. Teal is the accent for buttons, links and highlights. White space keeps it clean.

4

Three fonts, three jobs

Archivo for headlines. Public Sans for reading text. Arial in Word, Google Docs and email.

5

Warm, plain, a bit rock and roll

Write the way a friendly music teacher talks. Short sentences, real details, Australian spelling.

6

Real gear, real schools

Use real photos of instruments, workshops and handovers, and get permission before showing students or staff.

Putting rock instruments into school music rooms.

That's our tagline, and it's the test for everything we make. If a flyer, post or letter doesn't help get an instrument into a student's hands, simplify it until it does.

Logo

Space, size and placement

Give the logo room to breathe and keep it big enough to read.

Clear space

A
A

Keep a margin on every side at least as tall as the letter A in "AMPLIFY". Nothing else goes inside the dashed line: no text, no other logos, no page edges.

Smallest size

Print: 25 mm wide
Screen: 96 pixels wide

Any smaller and "SCHOOLS" becomes hard to read. Use the "A" icon instead.

Placement

  • Top left is the default, as on the website and letterhead.
  • Centre it only when it stands alone, such as on a cover, banner or profile picture.
  • Use one logo per page or screen. Repeating it weakens it.
  • Beside a partner's logo, keep both the same visual height with clear space between them.

Backgrounds

Colour logo on white or pale grey. White logo on navy or teal.

What not to do

The logo only works if it always looks the same. These are the mistakes that creep in most often.

✕
Don't stretch or squash it. Hold Shift while resizing so it keeps its shape.
✕
Don't change the colours. Use the colour, white or navy file.
✕
Don't rotate or tilt it. The italic lettering already has movement.
✕
Don't add effects. No shadows, glows, outlines or gradients.
✕
Don't use the colour logo on colour. Switch to the white logo so it stays readable.
✕
Don't crop it or pull it apart. The guitar and the words always stay together.

Type rules

  • Write headlines in sentence case, not Title Case or ALL CAPS.
  • Align text left. Centre only short headlines.
  • On screens, keep body text at 16 pixels or larger.
  • Don't use Archivo italic for paragraphs. It's for short lines only.
  • Don't add other fonts, even for special occasions.
Amplify Schools brand guidelines
Voice

How we sound

We sound like the people we are: music lovers who fix guitars and get them to schools. Friendly, clear and never corporate.

Warm

Talk to one person. Use "we" and "you". Say thanks often and mean it.

Plain

Short sentences and everyday words. Say exactly what happens to a donated guitar, and skip the jargon.

A bit rock and roll

A little band talk goes a long way: "Meet the band", "backstage", "keeping the amps humming". One riff per page is plenty.

Respectful

Schools, teachers and students are partners. We talk about what they can do with the gear, not what they lack.

House style

Spelling: Australian English (organise, colour, program).  Dates: 11 November 2026.  Money: $40, $150, $500.  Measurements: metric.  Instruments: guitars, basses, drums, keys and amps.  Exclamation marks: one at most, and rarely.

Amplify Schools brand guidelines
Photography

Photos

Real photos of real gear and real people are the most convincing thing we have. A phone photo of an actual handover beats a polished picture of nothing in particular.

A real handover at Narara Valley High School: the instrument, the people and the place, all in one shot.
Do
  • Show the instruments: on the workbench, in their cases, in students' hands.
  • Capture the three moments of our story: gear arriving, gear being fixed, gear being handed over.
  • Take before and after shots of refurbishments.
  • Use natural light and get close. Faces and hands tell the story.
  • Name the school and the people in the caption when you have their permission.
  • Keep the original, full-size file.
Don't
  • Don't publish a photo of a student without written consent from the school and a parent or guardian.
  • Don't publish photos of staff or volunteers without asking them first.
  • Don't use stock photos to stand in for our own work.
  • Don't present an AI-generated or illustrative image as a real school, student or event. Where a real photo exists, use it.
  • Don't add heavy filters, borders or text over faces.

Keep a record. Note who gave permission, for which photos, and when. If someone asks us to take a photo down, do it straight away.

Trust marks

Our charity details

Donors and schools look for proof that we're a genuine charity. Show these details clearly and word them the same way every time.

The Registered Charity Tick

The Tick belongs to the ACNC (Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission). We can use it while we stay registered, and only under the ACNC's rules:

  • Never recolour, redraw, stretch or add effects to it.
  • Keep it at least 15 mm wide in print, or 57 pixels on screen.
  • Leave 5 mm (19 pixels) of clear space around it.
  • Online, link it to our listing on the ACNC Charity Register.
  • Use the colour Tick on light backgrounds and the white Tick on dark ones.

Standard wording

Footer line
Letters, flyers, emails, receipts		Amplify Schools Incorporated | ABN 32 761 982 262 | Registered charity
RegistrationAmplify Schools is registered as a charity with the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC).

Being a charity is not the same as being tax deductible

Only organisations the ATO has endorsed as a Deductible Gift Recipient (DGR) can offer tax-deductible receipts. Until we have that endorsement, never write "tax deductible" or "tax-deductible receipt" in an appeal, post or receipt. When our status changes, update this page first.

Before you publish

  • Is our name written as "Amplify Schools"?
  • Is the logo an original file, unstretched, with clear space around it?
  • Are the colours our navy and teal, with plenty of white?
  • Does it read the way we'd say it out loud, in Australian English?
  • Do we have permission for every person in every photo?
  • Are the ABN and "Registered charity" shown where people expect them?
  • Have we avoided promising a tax deduction?
