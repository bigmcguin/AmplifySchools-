Why we exist

Not every school starts with the same resources, but every student deserves the same chances. Amplify Schools raises funds and builds partnerships so that school communities can afford the equipment, programs and extra support that make the biggest difference in a student's day.

We work directly with principals, teachers and families to put every dollar where it matters most.

What we do

Three ways we help schools do more for their students.

Classroom resources

We fund the books, technology and learning materials that stretched school budgets can't cover.

Student programs

Music, sport, wellbeing and enrichment programs that keep students engaged and connected.

Community partnerships

We connect schools with local businesses and volunteers who want to give back.
